About

I am a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Cerebras Systems. I do machine learning stuff.

My previous postdoc was at the Vector Institute, supervised by Sageev Oore, Before that, I was a postdoc in Morrislab, supervised by Quaid Morris. Before I came to Toronto, I completed my PhD with Amos Storkey in his research group at Edinburgh University. Our work focused on resource efficient machine learning and can be found in this list.

On AI risk, trust Hinton, Bengio and Duvenaud

There is also a longer term existential threat that will arise when we create digital beings that are more intelligent than ourselves. We have no idea whether we can stay in control.

Geoffrey Hinton, Nobel Prize banquet speech, 2024

If leading AI companies are indeed approaching the point of recursive self-improvement, a coordinated, verifiable, and universally applied pause is probably the only responsible solution to mitigate several major AI risks; at least until safety guarantees are developed and demonstrated.

Yoshua Bengio, June 2026

We argue that this dynamic could lead to an effectively irreversible loss of human influence over crucial societal systems, precipitating an existential catastrophe through the permanent disempowerment of humanity.

Kulveit, Douglas, Ammann, Turan, Krueger and Duvenaud, Gradual Disempowerment, 2025

I trust these sources because they are machine learning professors I have respected for over 10 years and I did not select them post hoc. I don't reiterate their arguments here but I have checked and they are correct.
